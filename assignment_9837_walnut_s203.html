Assignment of Real Estate Purchase and Sale Agreement

9837 Walnut St, Unit S203, Dallas, TX 75243 (Richland Trace Condominiums)

This Assignment (the “Assignment”) is made as of  , 2026, between Paragon Government Solutions LLC, a Virginia limited liability company (“Assignor”), and   (“Assignee”).

Recitals. Assignor is the Buyer under that certain Real Estate Purchase and Sale Agreement dated   (the “Purchase Agreement”) with the Seller of the property at 9837 Walnut St, Unit S203, Dallas, TX 75243 (the “Property”) at a purchase price of $86,500. Assignor desires to assign all of its rights and interest in the Purchase Agreement to Assignee, and Assignee desires to accept that assignment.

1. Assignment

Assignor hereby assigns, transfers, and conveys to Assignee all of Assignor’s right, title, interest, and equitable interest in and to the Purchase Agreement and the Property. Assignee accepts the assignment and assumes and agrees to perform all of Buyer’s obligations under the Purchase Agreement arising from and after the date of this Assignment, including closing on the terms of the Purchase Agreement.

2. Assignment Fee

In consideration of this Assignment, Assignee shall pay Assignor a non-refundable assignment fee of $  (the “Assignment Fee”), payable as follows: a non-refundable deposit of $  upon execution of this Assignment, with the balance paid to Assignor through the Closing Agent (title company) at, and as a condition of, closing. The Assignment Fee is fully earned upon execution and is non-refundable except for Assignor’s failure to deliver the assigned rights.

3. As-Is; No Warranty; Indemnification

Assignor assigns its interest AS-IS and makes no representation or warranty regarding the Property, the condominium/HOA, or the Purchase Agreement. Assignee has performed (or waived) its own due diligence, including review of HOA dues, the resale certificate, and any special assessments. Assignee shall indemnify, defend, and hold Assignor harmless from any claims, damages, or liabilities arising from the Property or Assignee’s performance of the Purchase Agreement after the date hereof.

4. Failure to Close

If Assignee fails to close under the Purchase Agreement, Assignee shall forfeit the deposit and Assignment Fee paid, and Assignor may, at its option, re-assign or enforce the Purchase Agreement. Assignee’s default shall not create liability for Assignor to the Seller.

5. Proof of Funds; General

Assignee shall furnish proof of funds upon request and is responsible for closing on the all-cash terms of the Purchase Agreement. This Assignment is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, binds the parties’ successors and assigns, may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, and constitutes the entire agreement regarding its subject matter. The prevailing party in any dispute recovers reasonable attorneys’ fees.
