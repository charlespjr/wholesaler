1. Property

Seller agrees to sell and Buyer agrees to buy the real property identified above, together with all improvements, fixtures, and appurtenances (the “Property”), legally described in the title commitment to be obtained. The Property is sold together with all mineral, water, and similar rights owned by Seller.

2. Purchase Price & Earnest Money

The total purchase price is the amount identified above, payable in cash or certified funds at Closing. Within five (5) business days after the Effective Date, Buyer shall deposit earnest money of $1,000.00 (the “Deposit”) with the Closing Agent, credited to the price at Closing and refundable to Buyer if this Agreement is terminated during the Inspection Period or for Seller default.

3. Closing

Closing shall occur within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date (unless extended in writing) at a title company or closing attorney selected by Buyer (the “Closing Agent”), as customary in the state identified above. Seller shall convey marketable, insurable title by general warranty deed, free of liens except those approved by Buyer, and shall pay for the owner’s title policy and release of existing liens.

4. Assignment

Buyer may freely assign, transfer, or convey this Agreement and all of Buyer’s rights and equitable interest, in whole or in part, to any person or entity without the consent of Seller. Seller acknowledges that Buyer is a real estate investor/wholesaler acquiring an equitable interest for resale or assignment and may profit from such assignment. Upon an assignment and the assignee’s assumption of Buyer’s obligations, the assigning Buyer is released from liability. This Agreement binds the parties’ heirs, successors, and assigns.

5. Marketing & Equitable Interest

Seller agrees that Buyer holds an equitable interest in the Property upon execution and may market and assign that contractual/equitable interest to prospective assignees and end buyers, subject to the State-Specific Provisions below. Buyer may record a memorandum or affidavit of this Agreement to give notice of Buyer’s equitable interest, and Seller consents to such recording.

6. Inspection / Due Diligence Period

Buyer shall have ten (10) days after the Effective Date (the “Inspection Period”) to inspect the Property, title, and all matters Buyer deems relevant. Buyer may terminate for any reason during the Inspection Period by written notice, in which case the Deposit is refunded and neither party has further obligation.

7. Condition of Property / “As-Is”

The Property is sold strictly AS-IS, WHERE-IS, with all faults. Buyer relies solely on its own inspections. Seller shall maintain the Property in its current condition through Closing.

8. Seller’s Representations and Warranties

Seller represents that: (a) Seller has full authority to sell; (b) there are no undisclosed liens, leases, tenancies, or litigation; (c) Seller has received no notice of code violations or special assessments except as disclosed; and (d) Seller is not a “foreign person” under FIRPTA (IRC §1445). These survive Closing.

9. Default and Remedies

If Buyer defaults, Seller’s sole and exclusive remedy is to terminate and retain the Deposit as agreed liquidated damages; Seller waives all other remedies, including specific performance and consequential damages. If Seller defaults or fails to convey marketable title, Buyer may terminate and recover the Deposit plus actual costs, or pursue specific performance.

10. Prorations, Risk of Loss, Possession

Taxes and assessments are prorated as of Closing. Risk of loss remains with Seller until Closing. Possession is delivered to Buyer at Closing, free of occupants unless otherwise agreed in writing.

11. Disclosures

Seller shall provide all property-condition and other disclosures required by the law of the state identified above and, for any dwelling built before 1978, the federal Lead-Based Paint Disclosure and EPA pamphlet.

12. State-Specific Provisions

The parties shall comply with all disclosure, licensing, and wholesaling requirements of the state identified above. Where that state restricts the marketing or sale of an equitable interest in real property, Buyer shall market and assign only its contractual/equitable interest in this Agreement (and not the Property itself) and shall provide any written wholesaler/equitable-interest disclosure required by that state. Required seller property-condition and lead-based-paint disclosures, where applicable, are incorporated by reference.

13. Notices

Notices shall be in writing to the parties at the addresses below. Buyer’s notice address: Paragon Government Solutions LLC, 11166 Fairfax, STE 500, Fairfax, VA 22030; phone 888.495.6935; email charles@paragongovsolutions.net.

14. General Provisions

Time is of the essence. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state identified above. The prevailing party in any dispute shall recover reasonable attorneys’ fees. This Agreement is the entire agreement, may be amended only in a signed writing, may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, and remains effective if any provision is severed.
