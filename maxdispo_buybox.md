# MaxDispo Buy-Box — what to feed them (and what NOT to)

> Learned the hard way 6/17/2026 when MaxDispo **rejected 7512 S Saint Lawrence**
> (contracted $110k, ~21% under list). Their words:
> *"We can't assign something contracted only 30k below list price. You'd need to
> be contracted at 60k or less before there's margin enough for us to collaborate."*

MaxDispo's buyers are **builders / developers**. They buy on a **deep-discount /
teardown / heavy-rehab basis**, NOT on retail or rental value.

## The rule
- **Contract price must be ~60% of value or LESS** (they need real builder spread).
- A house "30k under list" is still **retail** to them. Not enough margin.

## ✅ RIGHT product for MaxDispo
- Distressed / vacant / fire / gut-rehab / teardown
- Foreclosures, REO (if assignable), tax-delinquent, code-violation
- Contracted at **≤ 60% of list/ARV**
- Examples from our pipeline that FIT:
  - Detroit $11–25k batch (Keelie Thomas et al.)
  - Toledo — 3305 N Detroit Ave $17,500
  - Baton Rouge fixers — 3851 Uncas $12k, 2758 Badley $46.5k

## ❌ WRONG product for MaxDispo (route to buy-hold/landlord buyers instead)
- Turnkey / move-in-ready / recently remodeled
- Tenant-occupied rentals priced on income (cap rate)
- Coming-soon MLS / retail listings only lightly under list
- Example that did NOT fit: **7512 S Saint Lawrence** (remodeled 2020, tenant
  $1,500/mo) — a buy-hold deal, not a builder deal.

## Takeaway for sourcing
Two separate exit channels, two separate product types:
1. **MaxDispo (builders)** ← deep-discount distressed, ≤60% of value.
2. **Buy-hold / landlord buyers (self-dispo)** ← turnkey/rental, priced on cap rate.
Tag every contracted deal to the correct channel BEFORE submitting.
