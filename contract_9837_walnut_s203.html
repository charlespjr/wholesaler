Real Estate Purchase and Sale Agreement

(Cash — Assignable) • Condominium Unit

This Agreement is made as of  , 2026 (the “Effective Date”), between Seller:   (“Seller”), and Buyer: Paragon Government Solutions LLC, a Virginia limited liability company, and/or its assigns (“Buyer”).

Deal Terms

Property9837 Walnut St, Unit S203, Dallas, TX 75243 (Richland Trace Condominiums), together with the seller’s interest in all common elements, parking, and HOA membership (the “Property”).
Purchase Price$86,500.00, all cash, no financing contingency.
Earnest Money$1,000.00, deposited with the Closing Agent (title company) within three (3) business days after the Effective Date.
Closing DateOn or before   (target 7–10 days), subject to timely delivery of the HOA resale certificate.
Closing AgentA Texas title company selected by Buyer (“Buyer’s choice”).

1. Closing Costs (as negotiated)

Buyer shall pay the standard buyer-side and seller-side closing costs (title insurance, escrow/settlement fee, and recording fees), so that the transaction is a near-zero-cost settlement for Seller. Seller shall be responsible only for: (a) Seller’s prorated property taxes; (b) Seller’s prorated HOA dues and any HOA transfer/estoppel fee customarily charged to the seller; and (c) any real-estate brokerage commission owed under Seller’s listing agreement. The $86,500 Purchase Price is intended as a clean, near-“net” figure to Seller after the foregoing.

2. As-Is Condition

The Property is sold strictly AS-IS, WHERE-IS, with all faults. Buyer relies on its own inspection and not on any statement of Seller except the express representations herein.

3. Inspection / Due-Diligence Period

Buyer shall have a period of ten (10) days after the Effective Date to inspect the Property, review the HOA documents (Section 5), and evaluate all matters. Buyer may terminate this Agreement for any reason or no reason during this period by written notice, in which case the Earnest Money shall be promptly refunded in full and neither party shall have further obligation.

4. Assignment

Buyer may freely assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, to any person or entity without Seller’s consent, and may close by way of assignment or simultaneous (double) closing. Upon an assignee’s assumption of Buyer’s obligations, the assigning Buyer is released from liability.

5. Condominium / HOA

Within three (3) business days after the Effective Date, Seller shall request and provide to Buyer the HOA resale certificate / estoppel, current monthly dues, governing documents, and any special assessments. Buyer’s obligation to close is contingent on Buyer’s approval of these documents during the Inspection Period, and the Closing Date may be extended as reasonably necessary to obtain the resale certificate.

6. Title & Possession

Seller shall convey marketable, insurable title by general warranty deed, free of liens except those approved by Buyer. Possession is delivered to Buyer at Closing.

7. Default; Remedies

If Buyer defaults, Seller’s sole and exclusive remedy is to terminate and retain the Earnest Money as liquidated damages. If Seller defaults or fails to convey marketable title, Buyer may terminate and recover the Earnest Money plus actual costs, or pursue specific performance.

8. Proof of Funds; General

This is an all-cash purchase with no financing contingency; Buyer shall furnish reasonable proof of funds (Buyer’s or its assignee’s) upon Seller’s written request. Time is of the essence. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, and constitutes the entire agreement, amendable only in a signed writing. The prevailing party in any dispute recovers reasonable attorneys’ fees.
