1. Property

Seller agrees to sell and Buyer agrees to buy the real property and all improvements, fixtures, and appurtenances located at 3714 Benton Blvd, Kansas City, MO 64128, MO 64128 (County of  ________________ ) (the “Property”), legally described in Exhibit A attached hereto. The Property is sold together with all mineral, water, and similar rights owned by Seller.

2. Purchase Price

The total purchase price for the Property shall be $56,300 (the “Purchase Price”), payable in cash or certified funds at Closing.

3. Earnest Money Deposit

Within five (5) business days after the Effective Date, Buyer shall deposit earnest money of $1,000.00 (the “Deposit”) with the Closing Agent. The Deposit shall be credited to the Purchase Price at Closing and is refundable to Buyer if this Agreement is terminated during the Inspection Period or for any Seller default or failure of a condition.

4. Closing

Closing shall occur on or before  ________________  (and in any event within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date, unless extended in writing) at a title company or closing attorney selected by Buyer (the “Closing Agent”). Seller shall convey marketable, insurable title by general warranty deed, free of liens and encumbrances except those approved by Buyer. Seller shall pay for the owner’s title policy and the release of existing liens; remaining customary costs allocated per local practice.

5. Assignment

Buyer may freely assign, transfer, or convey this Agreement and all of Buyer’s rights, title, and equitable interest hereunder, in whole or in part, to any person or entity, without the consent of Seller. Seller acknowledges that Buyer is a real estate investor/wholesaler, that Buyer is acquiring an equitable interest in the Property for the purpose of resale or assignment, and that Buyer may profit from such assignment. Upon an assignment and the assignee’s assumption of Buyer’s obligations, the assigning Buyer shall be released from all liability under this Agreement. This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective heirs, successors, and assigns.

6. Marketing & Equitable Interest

Seller agrees that Buyer holds an equitable interest in the Property upon execution of this Agreement and may market the Property and/or Buyer’s contractual and equitable interest to prospective assignees and end buyers. Buyer may record a memorandum or affidavit of this Agreement to provide notice of Buyer’s equitable interest, and Seller consents to such recording.

7. Inspection / Due Diligence Period

Buyer shall have a period of  ten (10)  days after the Effective Date (the “Inspection Period”) to inspect the Property, title, and all matters Buyer deems relevant. Seller shall provide reasonable access. Buyer may terminate this Agreement for any reason or no reason during the Inspection Period by written notice, in which case the Deposit shall be promptly refunded to Buyer and neither party shall have further obligation.

8. Condition of Property / “As-Is”

The Property is sold strictly “AS-IS, WHERE-IS,” with all faults. Buyer relies solely on its own inspections and not on any statement of Seller except the express representations herein. Seller shall maintain the Property in its current condition through Closing and deliver it free of debris and personal property not conveyed.

9. Seller’s Representations and Warranties

Seller represents and warrants that: (a) Seller has full authority to sell the Property; (b) there are no undisclosed liens, leases, tenancies, or litigation affecting the Property; (c) Seller has received no notice of code violations, special assessments, or pending governmental actions except as disclosed; and (d) Seller is not a “foreign person” under FIRPTA (IRC §1445). These representations survive Closing.

10. Default and Remedies

If Buyer defaults, Seller’s sole and exclusive remedy shall be to terminate this Agreement and retain the Deposit as full and agreed liquidated damages, the parties acknowledging that actual damages would be difficult to determine; Seller waives all other remedies, including specific performance and consequential damages. If Seller defaults or fails to convey marketable title, Buyer may, at its option, (i) terminate and recover the Deposit plus Buyer’s actual costs, or (ii) pursue specific performance.

11. Prorations, Risk of Loss, Possession

Real estate taxes, assessments, and similar items shall be prorated as of Closing. Risk of loss remains with Seller until Closing. Possession shall be delivered to Buyer at Closing, free of occupants unless otherwise agreed in writing.

12. Disclosures

Missouri mandates no statutory form, but Seller must disclose known material latent defects; a Seller's Disclosure Statement is customary. For any dwelling built before 1978, Seller shall additionally provide the federal Lead-Based Paint Disclosure and EPA pamphlet. Seller shall make all other disclosures required by Missouri law.

13. State-Specific Provisions — Missouri

Closing practice. Missouri closings are customarily handled by a title company; the Closing Agent shall be a Missouri title company.

Wholesaling & assignment compliance. Assignment is permitted; disclose equitable interest and market the contract interest. Buyer represents it will comply with all Missouri licensing and disclosure requirements applicable to its acquisition and assignment of this Agreement.

14. Notices

Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, mail, or email to the parties at the addresses below. Buyer’s notice address: Paragon Government Solutions LLC, 11166 Fairfax, STE 500, Fairfax, VA 22030; phone 888.495.6935; email charles@paragongovsolutions.net.

15. General Provisions

Time is of the essence. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Missouri. In any dispute, the prevailing party shall recover reasonable attorneys’ fees and costs. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement, supersedes all prior understandings, and may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties. It may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original. If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder shall remain in effect.

3714 Benton Blvd, Kansas City, MO 64128 — complete only if the dwelling was built before 1978

Lead Warning Statement

Every purchaser of any interest in residential real property on which a residential dwelling was built prior to 1978 is notified that such property may present exposure to lead from lead-based paint that may place young children at risk of developing lead poisoning. Lead poisoning in young children may produce permanent neurological damage, including learning disabilities, reduced intelligence quotient, behavioral problems, and impaired memory. Lead poisoning also poses a particular risk to pregnant women. The seller of any interest in residential real property is required to provide the buyer with any information on lead-based paint hazards from risk assessments or inspections in the seller’s possession and notify the buyer of any known lead-based paint hazards. A risk assessment or inspection for possible lead-based paint hazards is recommended prior to purchase.
